How It Works

Three Steps. No Login.

Describe your idea in plain language. We evaluate it against real-world data and give you an honest verdict with a plan you can start today.

01

Tell Us Your Problem

Describe the problem you want to solve and who it affects. Be specific — "mothers in rural Kenya struggle to get homework help" beats "education is broken."

02

We Analyze It

We check cultural fit for your target country, match real case studies, map to UN Sustainable Development Goals, and score bootstrapper feasibility.

03

Get Your Verdict

A honest verdict: GO, GO WITH EDUCATION, PIVOT, or SHELVE. Plus a 14-day plan, funding options, and your first step — all for $0.
