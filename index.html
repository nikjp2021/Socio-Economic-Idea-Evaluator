What's behind it

165 organizations. 136 countries. Their patterns, your score.

We studied how real organizations solved real problems — then built an evaluator that checks your idea against what actually worked.

136 countries analyzed

For every country, we map the invisible forces that shape daily life — who makes decisions, how people relate to strangers, whether they plan for next year or next week. An idea that works in Denmark can fail in Nigeria. We explain why.

165 case studies

BRAC, Grameen, Aravind, and 162 more. Each one tells us what worked, what failed, and what conditions made the difference.

57 influential figures

Muhammad Yunus, Wangari Maathai, Iqbal Quadir — the people who proved that solving small problems can change countries. Their patterns shape our scoring.

6 cultural dimensions

Six forces that shape how people in every country live, work, and decide. An idea that ignores these is flying blind.

  • Power Distance — who gets to decide?
  • Individualism — me vs. we
  • Masculinity — competition vs. care
  • Uncertainty Avoidance — risk tolerance
  • Long-Term Orientation — now vs. later
  • Indulgence — restraint vs. freedom
