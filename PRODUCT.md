# PRODUCT.md — Socio-Economic Evaluator

## Register
brand

## Product Purpose
A free tool that evaluates social impact ideas through 7 layers of analysis. Tells you if your idea can work, what's in the way, and exactly what to do on Day 1. Based on research from 136 countries and 165 real organizations.

## Users
Social entrepreneurs in developing countries (India, Bangladesh, Kenya, Philippines). Not Silicon Valley founders. They are:
- Mothers running WhatsApp groups
- Farmers wanting fair prices
- Teachers helping girls stay in school
- Community health workers
- On budget Android phones (Redmi, Tecno, Samsung A-series)
- On 3G/4G connections
- English as second language
- $0 budget, solo, evenings only

## Brand Voice
- Honest, not cheerful. A partner who tells the truth, not a motivational speaker.
- Specific, not vague. "Find 3 mothers" not "reach out to your community."
- Plain language, not jargon. No "bootstrapper", "proof-of-work", "FAD risk", "Hofstede."
- Direct, not hedging. "This scored 7/10" not "you're closer than you think."
- Action-oriented, not descriptive. Every section ends with what to DO.

## Anti-References
- Shark Tank (trademarked, implies affiliation)
- Harvard (implies institutional credibility we don't have)
- Y Combinator (Silicon Valley jargon)
- Motivational speakers (hollow encouragement)
- Academic papers (jargon, inaccessible language)
- SaaS dashboards (not our audience)

## Strategic Principles
1. **Honest over encouraging.** A harsh PIVOT that saves 6 months is more valuable than a feel-good GO that leads nowhere.
2. **Specific over generic.** "Find 3 mothers in Dhaka" not "conduct user research."
3. **Plain over jargon.** A 16-year-old in Dhaka must understand every word.
4. **Action over analysis.** Every section answers "what do I do NOW?"
5. **Mobile-first.** Our users are on phones, not laptops.
6. **Free forever.** No paywalls, no sign-ups, no data collection.

## Tone Examples
**Do:** "Your idea scored 7 out of 10. One thing is stopping it: in your area, people follow leaders. Find a leader who supports you. That could add 1 point."

**Don't:** "Your idea has tremendous potential! You're closer than you think. The cultural matrix reveals some barriers, but with a 2-week education sprint, you can unlock significant upside!"

## Color Strategy
Committed: warm cream background (#faf7f2), forest green primary (#2d5a27), amber warnings (#c47a0a), terracotta alerts (#b5543a). The warmth signals "this is for you" not "this is a tech tool."

## Scene
A mother in rural Bangladesh sitting on her bed at 9pm, one hand holding her phone, the other holding her sleeping child. She has 15 minutes before sleep. She needs to know: will this idea work, and what do I do tomorrow?
